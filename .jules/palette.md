## 2024-05-24 - [Accordion Accessibility Patterns]
**Learning:** Custom accordion implementations (like the FaqSection) often miss critical ARIA attributes out of the box. Simply toggling a class for visibility isn't enough for screen readers. Screen readers need a deterministic way to understand the state (expanded/collapsed) and the relationship between the trigger and the content. Using generic buttons without these bindings makes the component act like a black box.
**Action:** When implementing or reviewing custom accordions or collapsible regions, always verify the presence of `aria-expanded` on the trigger button, `aria-controls` pointing to the content's ID, and `role="region"` with `aria-labelledby` on the content container. Also, ensure keyboard focus states (`focus-visible`) are clearly defined to support keyboard-only navigation.
